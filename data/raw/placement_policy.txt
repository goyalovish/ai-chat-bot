# Dr. B. R. Ambedkar National Institute of Technology Jalandhar
## Centre for Training and Placement (CTP) - Placement & Internship Policy (2026-2027)

### 1. Introduction & Overview
- Industrial training and internships are integral parts of the B.Tech curriculum under NEP-2020.
- B.Tech students undertake 3 to 4 weeks internship after 4th semester (compulsory, 2 credits), and 4 to 6 weeks internship after 6th semester (compulsory, 3 credits).
- Optional 6-month industry internship is permitted during the 7th or 8th semester (course waiver of 13 credits).
- M.Tech students are encouraged to pursue internships ranging from 6 months to 1 year.
- Securing an internship or placement is a shared effort between students, faculty coordinators, and CTP.

### 2. Placement Policy & Offer Rules
- Eligibility criteria are solely decided by the visiting organization; the Institute does not decide this.
- Registration on the CTP portal is mandatory. Unregistered students cannot sit for campus placements.
- Uncountable offers: IT company CTC <= 4.5 LPA and Core company CTC <= 3.5 LPA are not counted as final placement.
- One Student One Job Offer Policy: A student may avail at most one job offer through the institute, subject to exceptions.
- Placement drive phases: Phase I (June 2026 to 30 November 2026) and Phase II (01 December 2026 onwards).
- Ineligible after first offer: A student becomes ineligible for further drives immediately upon securing their first offer.
- Waitlist rule: If a waitlisted student gets an offer elsewhere, and later receives a confirmed offer from the waitlisted company, they may choose between the two on the same day.

### 3. Second Placement Offers & Dream Companies
- Second offer policy applies strictly during Phase II (commencing 01 December 2026).
- A student placed in Phase I (Company X) can sit for Company Y in Phase II only if:
  1. The CTC offered by Company Y is at least 1.5 times the CTC of Company X.
  2. At least 70% of registered students in the batch have already secured placements.
  3. The student has attended at least 80% of workshops, seminars, and training sessions conducted by CTP NIT Jalandhar.
- PSU Exclusion: Students placed in Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs) are considered finally placed and cannot participate in any further drives, including Dream companies.

### 4. Pre-Placement Offers (PPOs)
- Any student receiving an On-Campus or Off-Campus PPO must notify CTP on the same date via email (ctp@nitj.ac.in) and upload the offer letter to the portal.
- Concealing a PPO leads to immediate debarment from placements, potential disciplinary action, and withholding of NOC/relieving letters.
- A student holding a PPO from Company X may attempt ONE full-time offer (FTE) only if the new CTC is at least 1.5 times Company X's CTC.

### 5. Deferred Placement & Startups
- Students availing Technology Business Incubator (TBI) services for their own startups can defer placements up to 2 years after graduation.
- To enter placements, the student must submit a certificate from TBI stating they are no longer legally associated with the startup.

### 6. Full Semester (4-6 Months) Off-Campus Internships
- Permitted during 7th or 8th semester for up to 50% of the batch strength.
- Requirements for off-campus private companies:
  1. Company must be at least 3 years old.
  2. Company must have minimum sales of Rs. 1 Crore in the last financial year.
  3. Minimum monthly stipend of Rs. 25,000 for IT/non-core companies, and Rs. 10,000 for core companies.
  4. Students doing off-campus internships are strictly not permitted to stay in institute hostels, even with work-from-home options.
  5. Relieving certificate from CTP is mandatory before joining.

### 7. Full Semester Internship in One's Own Startup
- Eligibility requirements:
  1. Business must be registered as a Partnership, Pvt Ltd, or LLP during 3rd or final year.
  2. Valid PAN, GST, MSME registration, and Startup India Recognition certificate.
  3. Current bank account in the company's name.
  4. Students doing an internship in their own startup cannot appear for on-campus placements or internships during that period.

### 8. Disciplinary Rules & Dress Code
- Students must wear business formal attire during tests and interviews (shaved face, combed hair, proper attire).
- Mobile phones and electronic gadgets are strictly prohibited during tests; violations lead to immediate debarment.
- Impersonation or cheating leads to debarment from two or more recruitment drives or complete deregistration.
- Defamation or posting grievances on social media without resolving through CTP is prohibited and subject to legal defamation action.
- Leaving an internship midway without CTP permission leads to termination and award of 'W' grade.
- The Director of NIT Jalandhar is the final authority in all disputes.
